Privacy Policy — Lottokamu

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Lottokamu ("the app") is a Finnish lottery-results checker for Lotto, Eurojackpot, and Viking Lotto. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What the app collects

The app does not require an account, and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.

Lottery numbers you enter. When you check a row of numbers against a draw, the numbers and the game type are sent to our backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) to look up the matching draw results. This is not linked to any personal identity — there is no account or device identifier attached to it.

Camera photos (ticket scanning). If you use the camera to scan a physical ticket, the photo is processed entirely on your device using on-device text recognition. The photo and any text read from it are never uploaded or sent anywhere — they stay on your device and are discarded after the scan.

App preferences. Settings and previously entered rows may be saved locally on your device so they persist between app sessions. This data never leaves your device.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties
  • No user accounts or sign-in

Security

Data sent to our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The app uses Google Play Integrity / Apple App Attest to verify that requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app — this is a security check performed by Google/Apple and does not identify you personally.

Third-party services

The app's backend runs on Google Firebase (Cloud Functions and Firestore), hosted in the European Union (europe-west1).

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
